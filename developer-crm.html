DeveloperAscend Developer CRM · For developers

Your projects. Your buyers.

The CRM for real estate developers selling their own projects. Start with your buyers, your sales team and your site visits today; projects, units, pricing and reservations arrive in stages.

Start free Book a demo
Available today

Everything your sales team needs to work buyers.

Leads and buyers

Website forms, Excel and CSV imports in one list, each buyer with their own history.

Assignment and follow-ups

Assign by agent or team and track every follow-up against the buyer.

First-response tracking

See how long each enquiry waited for a first reply and get alerted when one waits too long.

Meetings and site visits

Visits and meetings on each buyer, with reminders so nobody forgets.

Team roles and permissions

Agents see their buyers; managers see the team.

HR, attendance and leave

The same HR, attendance and leave tools Ascend firms already use.

Coming next

Built around your units, not just your leads.

These modules arrive in stages. Each is marked until it is ready.

Projects, phases and buildings Coming soon

Your own projects, organised by phase, zone and building.

Unit inventory and availability Coming soon

Every unit tracked on its own, with live status from available to sold, and spreadsheet import.

Price lists, payment plans and quotations Coming soon

Versioned prices, installment plans and a quotation your buyer can take home, in English or Arabic.

Holds and reservations Coming soon

Hold or reserve a unit for a buyer, with expiry and approvals. A unit can never be reserved twice.

Contracts and collections Coming soon

Contracts from approved reservations, installment schedules and payment tracking.

Broker network and commissions Coming soon

The brokerages that sell for you, their agreements, referrals and commissions.

Developer dashboard Coming soon

Available, held, reserved and sold units, and sales by project and channel.
