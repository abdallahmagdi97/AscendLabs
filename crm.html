CAscend CRMLive

Every lead owned, followed up and accounted for.

Capture and assign leads, keep every follow-up and meeting on record, and give managers a clear view of the pipeline and the team behind it.

Book a demo Open CRM ↗

Features

Built around how sales teams actually work.

Lead capture & import

Add leads by hand or import spreadsheets, with duplicate checks before anything is saved.

Assignment & follow-ups

Assign leads to the right person and keep every call, note and status change on record.

First-response tracking

See how quickly new leads are contacted, with alerts when a lead waits too long.

Pipeline & deal stages

Move opportunities through clear stages from first contact to closed deal.

Meetings & reminders

Schedule online or in-person meetings, with reminders for your team.

Roles & reporting

Role-based access for agents, team leads and managers, with dashboards for each.

Workflow

From new lead to closed deal.

  1. 01

    Capture

    Leads arrive from forms, imports or your team.

  2. 02

    Assign

    Each lead gets an owner, and the response clock starts.

  3. 03

    Follow up

    Calls, notes, meetings and reminders stay on the lead’s timeline.

  4. 04

    Close & review

    Track deals by stage and review performance across the team.

Who uses CRM

Sales directors

Know where to step in

See which leads are waiting, which are going quiet and how each team is doing.

Team leads & agents

Never lose track of a lead

One list of what to do today, with history on every lead.

Business owners

A clear view of the pipeline

Lead, meeting and deal data recorded as work happens — not in spreadsheets.
