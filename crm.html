CAscend CRM · For the firm

No lead left waiting.

Every enquiry captured, assigned to the right agent and tracked to a closed deal, with managers seeing exactly who answered and how fast.

Book a demo See CRM pricing

Built for two kinds of sales team.

Brokerages

Route enquiries from your website, ads and imports to the right agent, follow every lead through to a viewing and a deal, and see which agents convert.

Developer sales teams

Keep your in-house team and partner brokers working the same leads, with every follow-up and meeting logged. Projects, units and payment plans Coming soon

Features

Built around how property sales teams actually work.

Lead capture & import

Website forms, Excel and CSV imports in one list. Facebook and Instagram lead ads Coming soon

Assignment & follow-ups

Assign by agent or team and track every follow-up against the lead. Automatic reassignment of unanswered leads Coming soon

First-response tracking & alerts

See how long each lead waited for a first reply and get alerted when one is waiting too long.

Pipeline & deal stages

A board from new enquiry to won deal, with stages your team recognises.

Meetings & reminders

Viewings and meetings on each lead, with reminders so nobody forgets.

Team roles & permissions

Agents see their leads; managers see the team.

Dashboards & reports

Pipeline, conversion and response time by agent and team.

WhatsApp click-to-chat Coming soon

Open a WhatsApp chat from the lead and keep a note of it on the timeline.

Mobile apps Coming soon

Ascend CRM on iOS and Android.
