Ascend Broker CRM · For brokerages

No lead left waiting.

Every enquiry captured, assigned to the right agent and tracked to a closed deal across every developer you sell for, with managers seeing exactly who answered and how fast.

Built for brokerages

Many developers, one pipeline.

01

Leads from every channel

Route enquiries from your website, ads and imports to the right agent, and follow every lead through to a viewing and a deal.

02

Developers and their projects

Keep the developers you sell for, their projects and their sales contacts in one place, linked to your deals and commissions.

Features

Built around how property sales teams actually work.

01

Lead capture & import

Website forms, Excel and CSV imports in one list. Facebook and Instagram lead ads are coming soon.

02

Assignment & follow-ups

Assign by agent or team and track every follow-up against the lead. Automatic reassignment of unanswered leads is coming soon.

03

First-response tracking & alerts

See how long each lead waited for a first reply and get alerted when one is waiting too long.

04

Pipeline & deal stages

A board from new enquiry to won deal, with stages your team recognises.

05

Meetings & reminders

Viewings and meetings on each lead, with reminders so nobody forgets.

06

Team roles & permissions

Agents see their leads; managers see the team.

07

Dashboards & reports

Pipeline, conversion and response time by agent and team.

08

WhatsApp click-to-chat Coming soon

Open a WhatsApp chat from the lead and keep a note of it on the timeline.

09

Mobile apps Coming soon

Ascend CRM on iOS and Android.
