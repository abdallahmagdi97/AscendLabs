Features

Everything you need to plan, send and learn from a campaign.

Campaign builder

Set up each campaign step by step: audience, content, preview, then send.

Audience selection

Choose recipients from your contacts using the filters your team already uses.

Templates & preview

Start from a template, personalise the message and preview it before it goes out.

Scheduling & test sends

Send now or schedule for later, and send yourself a test copy first.

Consent & unsubscribes
