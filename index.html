Put the right agent on it, fast

Assign by agent or team and follow every lead against its owner. On the Growth and Business plans, first-response tracking shows how long each lead waited and alerts you when one is waiting too long.

Follow through to the deal

A board of deal stages your team recognises, with meetings and reminders on each lead so nobody forgets the next step.

See the team, not just the leads

Pipeline, conversion and response time by agent and team. Standard reports on every plan; advanced dashboards on Growth and Business.

What you can rely on

Plain terms, stated up front.

Your leads stay yours

Leads in your firm’s Ascend CRM belong to the firm. Contacts an agent adds to their own Ascend Agent belong to the agent.

We move your data in

We import from Excel, CSV and most CRMs as part of onboarding.

One flat price per firm

Ascend CRM is priced per firm, not per seat: a 10-person team on Growth pays $49 a month in total.

Honest about what is not built yet

Anything still in development is marked Coming soon wherever it appears, including mobile apps, Arabic, Bookings and Facebook and Instagram lead ads.

Built in Cairo
