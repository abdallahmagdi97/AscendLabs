Acceptable Use Policy

Version 26 November 2026

Use of the services

Use Ascend Labs for lawful professional activities and respect the rights, privacy, and safety of others. Follow the Terms of Service, the requirements of any connected provider, and applicable law where you and your recipients are located.

Email, SMS, and other messages

Send messages only when you have the required permission or another lawful basis. Identify the sender, use accurate contact information and subject lines, and provide a workable opt-out where required. Honor opt-outs and suppression lists before sending again. Do not use scraped, purchased, or otherwise unauthorized lists. Do not send spam, deceive recipients, or route messages around provider rules or rate limits.

Data and content

Upload only records and content you have the right to use. Provide any required notices to people whose data you import or manage. Do not upload unlawful material, malware, or content that infringes another party’s rights. Restrict sensitive records to authorized users and to features appropriate for that information.

Security and platform integrity

Do not obtain unauthorized access, harvest credentials, evade safeguards, impersonate others, interfere with other customers, or send traffic that disrupts the services. Report suspected abuse or security issues to info@ascend-labs.cc. We may investigate and restrict the affected access or activity as described in the Terms.
