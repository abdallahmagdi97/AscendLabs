Refund Policy

Version 26 November 2026

Which terms apply

Your order or checkout sets out the cancellation and refund terms for your purchase. Mandatory rights under applicable law also apply. This policy explains how to request a review of a charge.

Canceling a subscription

Where your subscription renews automatically, cancel it before the next renewal to prevent a future charge. Use the available billing controls or contact support. A cancellation stops future renewals according to your plan. Eligibility for a refund of an existing charge is assessed separately.

Requesting a refund or correction

Email info@ascend-labs.cc with your account email, organization, invoice or transaction reference, charge date, and the reason for the request. We review requests, including duplicate or incorrect charges, against your order and applicable law. If we approve a refund, it is generally returned through the original payment provider. The provider may need additional time to post it.
