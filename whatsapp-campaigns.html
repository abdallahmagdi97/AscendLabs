Ascend Campaigns · WhatsApp

WhatsApp campaigns, checked before they send.

Send template messages to your own contacts from your own WhatsApp Business account. Every message is checked for consent, suppression and a valid number before anything is sent.

How it works

From connection to a checked send.

01

Connect WhatsApp

Connect your own WhatsApp Business account in Settings, so messages come from your own number.

02

Approve a template

WhatsApp campaigns use template messages. Create a template and get it approved before you send.

03

Record consent

Record consent for the contacts you message. Consent and suppression are checked on every send.

04

Create the campaign

Pick the template and the list, then send or schedule. A valid number is required for every recipient.

05

See what was delivered

Delivery status reports for every WhatsApp campaign.

Pricing

Priced per business, not per user.

Essentials is $29 a month for one number and 2 team members; Growth is $59 for 3 numbers and 5 team members; Business is $99 for 10 numbers and 15. Yearly billing gives you 2 months free.
