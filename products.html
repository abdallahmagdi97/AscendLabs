Sales software for real estate, one for the firm and one for the agent.

Ascend CRM is for brokerages and developer sales teams. Ascend Agent is for individual sales agents. Use either, or both.

Ascend CRM

For the firm

Capture every enquiry, assign it to the right agent, and give managers a live view of the pipeline and the team behind it.

  • Lead capture & import
  • Assignment & follow-ups
  • First-response tracking & alerts
  • Pipeline & deal stages
  • Meetings & reminders
  • Team roles & permissions
  • Dashboards & reports
  • Facebook & Instagram lead ads Coming soon
  • WhatsApp click-to-chat Coming soon
