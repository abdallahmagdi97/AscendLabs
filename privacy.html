1. Who we are

1.1 This Privacy Policy explains how Ascend Labs (“Ascend”, “we”, “us”) handles personal data on ascend-labs.cc and in our products: Ascend CRM (app.ascend-labs.cc) and Ascend Agent, which includes Follow-Up (followup.ascend-labs.cc), Bookings (bookings.ascend-labs.cc, in development) and Campaigns (campaigns.ascend-labs.cc).

1.2 Ascend Labs is operated by [TO CONFIRM: legal entity name] at [TO CONFIRM: registered address], Cairo, Egypt. Contact: info@ascend-labs.cc.

2. Our roles: controller and processor

2.1 Ascend as controller. We decide how and why we use data about: people who contact us through the website, our account holders and users, and billing. This policy mainly describes that data.

2.2 Ascend as processor. Our customers (for example brokerages, developer sales teams and agents) use the products to store and use their own leads, contacts, deals and campaign recipients (“Customer Data”). The customer is the controller of Customer Data and Ascend is its processor. We process it only to provide the Services and on the customer’s instructions. If you are a person whose data is in a customer’s account, please contact that customer first about your rights. You can also write to us and we will pass the request on.

3. What we collect, why, and our legal basis

The legal bases below are a neutral draft for review. [TO CONFIRM: legal bases per purpose, under Egypt’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020 and, where applicable, the EU/UK GDPR]

  1. Website contact form: name, work email, company, role, product interest, your message, an optional marketing opt-in, campaign (UTM) parameters, and the page you sent it from. Used to reply to you, arrange demos, and understand which pages and campaigns lead to enquiries. Basis: our legitimate interest in handling enquiries, or steps you ask us to take before a contract; marketing messages only with your consent.
  2. Account data: name, email, phone, role, organization, and sign-in records. Used to create and secure accounts and provide the Services. Basis: performing our contract with the customer; our legitimate interest in security.
  3. Billing data: billing contact, invoice details and payment status. Once paid plans launch online, card payments are processed by Polar and card details are handled by Polar, not by us. Basis: contract and legal obligations (such as tax and accounting).
  4. Product data customers add (Customer Data): leads, contacts, deals, notes, follow-ups, meetings, and similar. Used to provide the Services. Basis: set by the customer as controller.
  5. Email campaign data: recipients’ email addresses and delivery events (sent, delivered, bounced, complaints, unsubscribes), used to deliver campaigns and honour suppression. Processor role.
  6. WhatsApp data (only if a customer connects WhatsApp): recipients’ phone numbers, consent records, message status, and the encrypted access tokens for the customer’s own WhatsApp Business account. Processor role.
  7. Technical and security data: IP address (used for rate limiting and security), and security audit logs. Basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the Services secure and preventing abuse.
  8. Correspondence: emails, WhatsApp messages and support requests you send us. Basis: legitimate interest and contract.

4. How we use data

4.1 To provide, secure, support and improve the Services; to reply to enquiries; to send service messages (such as account and billing notices); to prevent fraud and abuse and enforce our Acceptable Use Policy; to meet legal obligations; and, with your consent, to send you marketing, which you can withdraw at any time.

4.2 We do not sell personal data. We do not use Customer Data for our own marketing. [TO CONFIRM: any use of data to improve the product or for analytics, and any use for automated decision-making or AI features]

5. Email and WhatsApp specifics

5.1 Email campaigns are delivered through Resend. Each customer is responsible for having consent or another lawful basis to email its recipients. We process unsubscribes and suppression lists so that opted-out recipients are not emailed again.

5.2 WhatsApp campaigns use the customer’s own WhatsApp Business account through Meta’s official Cloud API. Messages and related data pass through Meta’s WhatsApp Business Platform, which handles them under its own terms and privacy notices. We store the customer’s access tokens in encrypted form. The customer is responsible for obtaining and recording recipients’ opt-in, and for honouring opt-outs.

We share data only with service providers that help us run the Services, and where the law requires. They act on our instructions or the customer’s:

  • Vercel: website and web app hosting.
  • Railway: API hosting. Database host: [TO CONFIRM: database provider and region].
  • Resend: transactional and campaign email delivery.
  • Zoho: company mailboxes (for example info@ascend-labs.cc).
  • Cloudflare: DNS, and Turnstile bot checks on forms if enabled.
  • Meta Platforms: WhatsApp Business Platform, only for customers who connect WhatsApp.
  • Polar: payment processing, once paid plans launch. This is not live today.

We may also disclose data to professional advisers, to a buyer in a business sale, or to authorities where the law requires. [TO CONFIRM: processor contract and sub-processor change-notice arrangements]
