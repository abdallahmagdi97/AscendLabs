Privacy Policy

Version 26 November 2026

Who this policy covers

This policy covers visitors, account holders, and users of Ascend Labs websites, Ascend CRM, and Ascend Agent (including Campaigns). Ascend Labs Programs LLC is the data controller for its own account administration, billing, security, and communications. When an organization uses our services to manage its own contacts, staff, or recipients, that organization generally acts as the controller and we handle workspace data as its processor. Contact that organization first about its use of your data. You can reach Ascend Labs Programs LLC at info@ascend-labs.cc or at Steps, 5th Settlement, Cairo, Egypt.

Information we handle

We handle account and organization details such as names, business contact details, account roles, credentials or their protected representations, and policy acceptance records. We also handle support and demo enquiries, subscription and transaction records, and technical information such as device or browser details, IP addresses and security events. Workspace content may contain customer or prospect details, contact lists, campaign messages and delivery events, notes, files, and meeting or staff records supplied by the customer. Connected services provide the data and permissions described when you enable them.

Why we use it

We use information to provide requested services, authenticate users, administer workspaces, deliver messages, manage subscriptions, help with support, protect against abuse, and fulfill legal obligations. Where applicable law permits, processing may rely on providing the service or responding to your request, a legal obligation, a legitimate operational interest, or your consent. We request a separate choice for promotional email. Account notices, sign-in messages, and service or billing communications are sent as needed to operate the service.

Cookies, security checks, and analytics

We use browser storage and similar technologies to remember sessions and preferences and to protect the service. Cloudflare Turnstile may process browser and network signals to check for automated activity. Where enabled, analytics and referral data help measure site usage. When a customer enables campaign link tracking, the service records visits to tracked links and groups suspected automated clicks separately. Browser settings and controls presented on our sites can help you manage optional technologies.

Connected Google Calendar and Meet

When you connect Google, we use the linked account information and authorization tokens to identify the connection and create, update, or remove calendar events you manage through CRM. This can include meeting times, details, attendee addresses, event identifiers, and Meet links. If you choose to send an invitation, attendee details are shared with Google so it can deliver it. We store connection credentials on the server and keep event identifiers and meeting links as needed to manage those meetings. You can disconnect in CRM or revoke access in your Google Account. Events already in Google Calendar can be managed there. Our use and transfer of Google data follows the Google Workspace user data policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We use Google data for the connected feature and exclude it from advertising and sale.

Service providers and disclosures

We share information as needed with hosting and infrastructure providers, security and analytics providers, email or messaging delivery services, payment processors, and integrations you configure. When enabled, Anora may send your question and relevant context to its configured AI provider to generate an answer. Workspace administrators can access workspace records within their permissions. We may disclose information when required by law or to address a security incident, prevent abuse, or protect legal rights. Providers may operate in different countries. We follow applicable requirements for international transfers.

Retention and security

Retention depends on the type of information, the applicable workspace agreement, and legal or security needs. We retain account and workspace information to provide the service and may keep relevant records after closure for billing, legal compliance, security, disputes, or backup recovery. We use access controls, authentication, and other safeguards designed to protect personal data. You can contact us about retention, export, or deletion of specific records.

Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may be entitled to access, correct, obtain a copy of, delete, or restrict use of your personal data, or object to certain processing. You may withdraw consent at any time. Use the unsubscribe link in a promotional email or contact us to stop promotions. Withdrawing marketing consent does not affect your account. Email info@ascend-labs.cc to make a privacy request. We may verify your identity and refer workspace requests to the responsible organization. Where available, you may also complain to the relevant data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

We publish updates with a new version date and provide further notice or request consent when required.
