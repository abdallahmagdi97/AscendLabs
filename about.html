About Ascend Labs

Useful software, built for the way property teams actually work.

Ascend Labs builds sales software for real estate. We started with brokerages in Egypt and the Gulf, and we build every feature with the teams who use it.

Ascend CRM is for the firm and Ascend Agent is for the individual agent. Both are approachable on day one and dependable on day one thousand.

How we build

01

Product thinking first

We start with the job a team needs done, then remove everything that gets in the way of doing it.

02

Engineering quality

Permissions enforced on the server, careful data handling and tests that protect the things that matter.

03

Practical workflows

Features earn their place by saving time in a real working day — not by filling a comparison chart.

04

Approachable by design

Clear screens, sensible defaults and plain language, so teams adopt the tools quickly.
