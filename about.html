About Ascend Labs

Useful software, built for the way businesses actually work.

Ascend Labs builds business software that is approachable on day one and dependable on day one thousand. We start from real workflows — a lead that needs a call, a campaign that needs to go out — and design focused products around them.

Each product stands on its own, so you only adopt what you need. Together they form a growing suite with a shared standard of design and engineering.

How we build

01

Product thinking first

We start with the job a team needs done, then remove everything that gets in the way of doing it.

02

Engineering quality

Permissions enforced on the server, careful data handling and tests that protect the things that matter.

03

Practical workflows

Features earn their place by saving time in a real working day — not by filling a comparison chart.

04

Approachable by design

Clear screens, sensible defaults and plain language, so teams adopt the tools quickly.
