Acceptable Use Policy

This Acceptable Use Policy applies to everyone who uses Ascend CRM and Ascend Agent (Follow-Up, Bookings and Campaigns). It forms part of our Terms of Service. The customer is responsible for the use of its accounts by its team.

1. Sending email

1.1 Consent. Send marketing email only to people who have given consent, or where another lawful basis applies under the laws that cover you and your recipients. Keep records of how and when you got consent.

1.2 Unsubscribe. Every marketing email must include a working unsubscribe link, and you must act on opt-outs promptly. [TO CONFIRM: maximum time to process opt-outs; suggested: no more than 10 days, ideally immediately]

1.3 Sender identity. Use your real name or business name and a real reply address. Do not use misleading subject lines or headers, or pretend to be someone else.

1.4 Postal address. Include your business’s physical postal address in marketing emails where the law requires it.

1.5 No purchased or scraped lists. Do not email lists you bought, rented or scraped.

1.6 Suppression. Do not re-add or email people on a suppression or unsubscribe list. We honour suppression, and you must not work around it.

2. Sending WhatsApp messages

2.1 Opt-in. Message people on WhatsApp only after they have opted in. The opt-in must clearly tell them your business name and what kind of messages they will get.

2.2 Opt-outs. Honour opt-out requests promptly and do not message people who have asked to stop. Do not send marketing messages to people who have stopped receiving marketing messages.

2.3 WhatsApp policies. Follow WhatsApp’s Business Messaging Policy and the other terms of Meta that apply to your account.

2.4 Templates and the 24-hour window. Outside the 24-hour customer-service window after a person’s last message, send only message templates approved by WhatsApp.

2.5 Your account. WhatsApp campaigns run on your own WhatsApp Business account. You are responsible for your own consent records and for the standing and quality rating of your account. Meta may limit or suspend your account independently of us.

3. Prohibited content and conduct

3.1 You must not use the Services to send, store or share:

  • anything illegal, or that encourages illegal acts;
  • fraud, scams or deceptive offers, including fake listings or advance-fee schemes;
  • phishing or attempts to collect passwords or financial details by deception;
  • malware, viruses or harmful code;
  • harassment, threats, hate or abuse;
  • adult sexual content;
  • content that infringes others’ rights, or that you do not have the right to use.

4. Security and fair use

4.1 Do not probe, scan or test the security of the Services, or access accounts or data that are not yours.

4.2 Do not scrape or bulk-extract data from the Services except through features we provide.

4.3 Do not circumvent or try to avoid rate limits, sending limits, plan limits or other protections, or disrupt the Services or other customers.

4.4 Do not share credentials in a way that breaches your plan’s user limits.

5. Enforcement

5.1 We may review reports and, where needed to protect people or the Services, check sending patterns and complaint, bounce and opt-out rates. [TO CONFIRM: sending-quality thresholds, for example complaint or bounce rates that trigger a review]

5.2 If we find or reasonably suspect a breach, we may warn you, limit features or suspend sending (email, WhatsApp or both), or suspend the account. We will give notice where possible. We may act immediately, without notice, for abuse, fraud, security risk or where required by law or by a provider such as Resend or Meta.

5.3 Suspending an organization or its sending does not delete its data by itself. Data is kept as described in the Privacy Policy and Terms.

5.4 Repeated or serious breaches can lead to termination under the Terms. Where we suspend for a breach, a refund is not automatic: see the Refund Policy.

5.5 We may report illegal activity to the authorities.

6. Reporting abuse

6.1 If you received an unwanted message sent through Ascend, or you see a breach of this policy, email info@ascend-labs.cc with the message or details. We will review it and take appropriate action. [TO CONFIRM: target response time for abuse reports; suggested: within 2 business days]

7. Changes

7.1 We may update this policy as laws, providers’ rules or our Services change. For material changes we will give notice as set out in the Terms.
